MLB Skewed Distribution Forecast

Padovani skewed-distribution algorithm (v2.1.2025) ported from FRC to Major League Baseball. Team strength = per-game wOBA ÷ per-game FIP, indexed to league = 100.

Demo data. These numbers come from scripts/make_synthetic.py, not the MLB Stats API. Run the refresh workflow to replace them with real games.

1 · Team strength ratings

Mode is the damped most-probable strength: the intersection of the scaled normal and scaled skew-adjusted curves between their peaks. Band is the variance bound from the outer two intersections. Skew shows the value before and after the 10%/10% pre-filter — watch how much signal the trim removes.
